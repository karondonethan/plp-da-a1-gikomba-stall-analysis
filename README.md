## Business problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. For two weeks she wrote her daily sales in a notebook and kept a rough record of her stock. She wants to know how her business is doing, whether it is growing, what she should restock, and where she should focus her effort. This project answers those questions using core Python only (no pandas or NumPy): totals, week-on-week growth, strong/weak day classification, weekday vs weekend comparison, reorder alerts and gross margins, ending with a formatted performance report and recommendations.

## How to run

[![Open In Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/assets/colab-badge.svg)](https://colab.research.google.com/github/karondonethan/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb)

Click the badge, then choose **Runtime → Run all**.

## Top three findings

1. **The business is growing:** sales rose from KES 22,050 in week 1 to KES 24,400 in week 2, a **+10.7%** week-on-week increase (KES 46,450 total, KES 3,317.86 average per day).
2. **Weekends drive the business:** Sat/Sun average **KES 4,900** per day versus **KES 2,685** on weekdays, which is **82.5% more**, and all 4 "Strong" days were weekends. Gikomba Stall Analysis
